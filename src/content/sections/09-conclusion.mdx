State management architecture is easiest to understand by starting with the primary design unit:

**Store-centric systems** organize state around feature-owned stores, reducers, ViewModels, or BLoCs. They are explicit, structured, and easy to trace.

**Atomic / Graph-centric systems** organize state around small composable values: atoms, providers, signals, or nodes. They make derived state first-class and often capture dependencies automatically from reads.

Push and pull still matter, but they are lower-level evaluation semantics. The real evolution is: **derived state dependency management is becoming increasingly explicit and automated**, whether the surrounding architecture is store-centric, atomic, or signals-style.
